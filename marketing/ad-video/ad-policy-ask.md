# Asking the ad platforms in writing (draft for the owner)

Status: **proposal**. The plan is organic posting first (CEO, 2026-11-19). The owner decides whether to send these questions and
from which account. No message has been sent. Policy quotes and links: `research-2026-11.md` §4.

## How Yondo describes itself (truthful, the same text to every platform)
> Yondo is a mobile and web platform in Uzbekistan where adults (18+) book time with an approved companion for a shared everyday
> activity in a public place: coffee, a walk in the city, cinema, an event or a conversation. Companions publish their services,
> languages and their own prices; customers send a booking request that the companion accepts or declines; the platform takes a
> commission. It is not a dating, matchmaking or escort service: there is no matching, no romantic categories in ads, and meetings
> are recommended in public places. Users can report and block through a booking, and moderators review reports.

Rules for this text: say that companions are **paid** (never hide it); do not call companions "tour guides" or "friends"; do not
claim safety guarantees, verification of every user, or that SOS sends help.

## Questions
**Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — Ads support or the dating-ads permission form**
1. Is an activity-companion booking platform (paid companion time, no matchmaking) treated as a "dating service" that needs prior
   written permission, as a "social app without matchmaking elements" (allowed), or as prohibited "dating based on financial
   transactions"?
2. If permission is needed, may we apply with the description above, targeting 18+ in Uzbekistan?
3. Are creatives showing two adults of the same gender, or of clearly different ages, doing an activity (museum, walk) acceptable?

**TikTok — via a TikTok for Business representative (the policy says "companionship … in exchange for compensation" is prohibited)**
1. Does that rule cover a platform where adults book a companion for a public activity (coffee, walk, event) for a fee set by the
   companion?
2. If yes: is there any compliant way to advertise such a platform when the ad states truthfully that companions are paid?
   If the answer is no, we stay organic on TikTok (we never run ads that hide the paid model).
3. Do the rules for organic brand accounts (non-ad posts) differ?

**Google / YouTube — Google Ads support, Dating and Companionship certification**
1. Does Yondo fall under "Dating and Companionship" and need certification, or under travel/experiences (local hosts)?
2. Is it "compensated companionship" (prohibited) when companions charge for their time for public activities and no
   dating or sexual element exists?
3. If certification is possible: which documents are needed for a business registered in Uzbekistan?
