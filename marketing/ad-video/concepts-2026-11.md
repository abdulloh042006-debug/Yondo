# Ad video 5 — concepts (Stage 1, 2026-11-19)

One idea for every concept: **"Yondo — doim yoningda."** Yondo = a real person to share an activity with (kafe, sayr, muzey, kino,
tadbir, hobbi). Research and policy basis: `research-2026-11.md`. Sample production pack for the top concept:
`flow-pack-sample-reja.md` (+ `.html` for the phone).

Rules applied to every concept: no romance, no dating grammar (no swipe-to-match, no couples at sunset, no candles), no
"cures loneliness", no health claims, no guaranteed friendship, no "100% xavfsiz", no prices or discounts, no partner venues,
no "til mashqi", 18+ adults only, friendly gestures only (handshake, wave), **real app footage only**, captions ON (1995 Latin:
o‘ g‘ sh ch, tutuq ʼ). Casting tip used throughout: pairs are **same-gender or clearly different ages/roles** (a guide-like host and a
guest), and every two-person shot keeps at least an arm's length; that removes the "date" reading without saying "not dating".

Cost categories: **0** = free (our pipeline only) · **L** = a few Flow generations (≤ 10 video takes) · **M** = 10–25 takes · **H** = real
shoot or many takes.

## C1. «Keyinroq» ro‘yxati — The unused plan  ★ rank 1
- **Insight:** everyone keeps a list of things they mean to do in their own city (the new exhibition, the old town walk, the chess club).
  Plans stay unused not for lack of time but for lack of someone to go with. *(Interpretation; matches Timeleft's routine-hook
  learning and Meta's rule: we describe a list, not the viewer.)*
- **Audience:** 20–35, city residents (Toshkent first), students and young professionals; secondary: people new to the city.
- **Emotional hook:** recognition with a smile ("my list looks like that"), then relief: one item gets a tick.
- **First 3 s:** a hand-written-style list on paper ground: "Yangi ko‘rgazma", "Eski shahar bo‘ylab sayr", "Kino festivali",
  "Shaxmat klubi". A carrot stamp «KEYINROQ» hits each item on the beat (4 hits in 2.4 s); the date in the corner rolls
  Sentabr → Oktabr → Noyabr. Caption: «Bu ro‘yxat hammada bor.»
- **Story:** list → two short AI shots of the items still waiting (a passer-by slows at an exhibition poster and walks on; a chess
  board on a shelf) → question «Kim bilan?» → real app: find a companion by interest → profile interests → booking request →
  "Bron so‘rovi yuborildi" → AI payoff: the same person in the museum hall with a companion who points something out; chess in the
  park ends with a handshake → the list again, items get ticks one by one → end card.
- **Visual:** paper/ink motion type with carrot only as stamp and tick; AI shots neutral daylight grade; real phone in the kit mockup.
- **Motion / AI / app:** motion 50 % (list, stamps, transitions, end card) · AI 30 % (4 shots) · app 20 % (4 screens).
- **Difficulty:** medium. **Cost:** L (4 shots × ~2–3 takes + 6 reference images). **Platforms:** Reels, TikTok, Shorts (9:16), Stories cut 15 s.
- **Why it works:** concrete object hook, positive ending, shows the activity before the app, one sentence to remember
  («Rejangiz bor. Yondo — sherik.»), and the list format makes endless variants (new item = new ad).

## C2. Shaharingni boshqacha ko‘r — Your city, differently  ★ rank 2
- **Insight:** you walk the same street every day and never see it; a local who loves it shows you a door, a courtyard, a tea house.
- **Audience:** residents bored with their routine; visitors, students from the regions, expats.
- **Emotional hook:** curiosity ("I never noticed that").
- **First 3 s:** split screen, the same street: left side greyscale fast-forward autopilot commute, right side normal speed, a hand
  points up at a carved wooden door; caption «Har kuni o‘tasiz. Ko‘rganmisiz?»
- **Story:** three "details" revealed by a companion (door, mosaic, a quiet courtyard), each one becomes a line-art pin on a motion
  map; the map folds into the app's list filtered by interest "Sayr"; profile; request; end card «Shahar o‘sha. Siz boshqacha ko‘rasiz.»
- **Visual:** AI street shots in late-morning light, line-art map in brand colours.
- **Mix:** motion 45 % · AI 35 % · app 20 %. **Difficulty:** medium-high (AI city detail consistency). **Cost:** M.
- **Platforms:** all; strong for YouTube Shorts and a 16:9 version.
- **Why it works:** sells an experience (Withlocals/Showaround learning), invites a second watch for details; risk: AI may invent
  real-looking landmarks: use generic Tashkent-like architecture, no named buildings.

## C3. Yondo nima? (10 soniyada) — What Yondo is, in 10 seconds  ★ rank 3
- **Insight:** people cannot place a new category; ambiguity makes them guess "dating?". Clarity is the ad.
- **Audience:** everyone seeing Yondo for the first time; also useful as a pinned post and for platform reviewers.
- **Emotional hook:** a quick, confident explainer rhythm.
- **First 3 s:** big word «YONDO» splits into three cards: «Faoliyat» (activity), «Hamroh» (a real person), «Jamoat joyi» (public place).
- **Story:** card 1 shows activities (motion icons: cup, walk, museum column, film strip, chess); card 2 shows a real profile in the app
  (illustrated portrait, interests); card 3 shows a cafe/park (AI or line-art). One line only: «Tanishuv ilovasi emas — faoliyat uchun
  hamroh.» Then the honest how-to: «Profil → so‘rov → hamroh tasdiqlaydi.» Safety as it exists: «Shikoyat va bloklash bron orqali.» End card.
- **Mix:** motion 70 % · AI 0–10 % · app 25 %. **Difficulty:** low. **Cost:** 0–L. **Platforms:** all; pinned post; 16:9 for YouTube.
- **Why it works:** cheapest, fastest, pre-empts the wrong reading; good for policy review screenshots.

## C4. Bo‘sh stul — The empty seat (rank 4)
- **Insight:** activities are built for two or more: a chess table has two chairs, a bench two ends, a tandem kayak two seats.
- **First 3 s:** top shot of a chess table: one chair taken, the other empty, the clock already ticking.
- **Story:** four "empty second places" (chess chair, second museum audio-guide headset, second cinema-club seat, second bicycle in a
  rental rack) → app → the seats fill with a companion (handshake, wave) → end card. Avoid two-seat cinema/candle-like settings.
- **Mix:** motion 40 · AI 40 · app 20. **Difficulty:** medium. **Cost:** M. **Why:** a single strong visual motif, loops well. Risk: empty
  chair = sad image; keep it playful (the ticking clock, the beat).

## C5. Shahar odamlarga to‘la — A city full of people (rank 5)
- **Insight:** the city is full of people who like the same things you do; you just never meet them for an activity.
- **First 3 s:** a crowd timelapse turns into thousands of paper dots on ink; a few dots light carrot with tiny icons (book, chess, camera).
- **Story:** two dots with the same icon draw a carrot line to a place pin (museum) → app list filtered by interest → request → end card.
- **Mix:** motion 75 · AI 10 · app 15. **Cost:** 0–L. **Why:** data-viz energy, no faces needed. Risk: close to reel 1's night city:
  must look different (top-down map, daylight).

## C6. Faoliyat, svayp emas — An activity, not a swipe (rank 6)
- **Insight:** apps train the thumb to swipe faces; Yondo starts from what you want to do.
- **First 3 s:** a thumb swipes; the swipe trail becomes a walking path on a map.
- **Story:** "swipe" gestures morph into activities (swipe → path, tap → ticket, scroll → museum hall pan) → the real card feed with
  «Saqlash» / «Keyingisi» (no match mechanic) → request.
- **Mix:** motion 60 · AI 20 · app 20. **Cost:** L. **Why:** sharp positioning. **Risk:** it puts the dating frame into the viewer's head
  and the app does have a card feed: rank low; use only if the CEO wants explicit positioning.

## C7. Bitta kichik qaror — One small decision (rank 7)
- **Insight:** the decision is small (one request), the day it opens is not.
- **First 3 s:** a finger hovers over «So‘rov yuborish», time slows (real button, real screen).
- **Story:** split-second flash-forward of the activity (AI), back to the tap, success toast.
- **Mix:** motion 40 · AI 30 · app 30. **Cost:** L. **Why:** app-centred. **Risk:** too close to reel 3 «Birinchi qadam»: needs a fresh angle.

## C8. Shanba, 15:00 — The weekend slot (rank 8)
- **Insight:** an empty Saturday in the calendar.
- **First 3 s:** a calendar week, Saturday blank, cursor blinking.
- **Story:** blocks drop into Saturday: «Muzey · 15:00», each block opens into a 2 s AI shot; ends in the app's Bookings list.
- **Mix:** motion 55 · AI 25 · app 20. **Cost:** L. **Why:** planning frame, positive. **Risk:** overlaps C1; can be a C1 variant.

## C9. Mehmon — The visitor (rank 9)
- **Insight:** first week in a new city (student, specialist, tourist): the map is clear, the city is not yet yours.
- **First 3 s:** suitcase wheels on a station platform, departure board in line-art.
- **Story:** a local companion shows tea house, metro mosaics, bazaar (AI), app list in RU/EN as well as UZ (real locale switch) → end card.
- **Mix:** motion 35 · AI 45 · app 20. **Cost:** M. **Why:** clear target; RU/EN cut natural. **Risk:** looks like a tour company;
  must not promise "guided tours" or licences.

## C10. Qiziqishlar xaritasi — Interests as objects (rank 10)
- **Insight:** an interest is a physical object: a chessboard, a film camera, a sketchbook, a ball.
- **First 3 s:** objects drop onto a paper table in rhythm, each with a label.
- **Story:** each object match-cuts to the real "Qiziqishlar" chips on a profile, then to an AI moment using it together.
- **Mix:** motion 60 · AI 25 · app 15. **Cost:** L. **Risk:** close to reel 2's object match-cuts.

## Production approach
**Start mix 60 / 25 / 15 → recommended 50 / 30 / 20** (motion / AI / real app), because:
- reels 1–3 were ~85 % motion and still read as "animation about an app"; the human activity shots are the missing proof of the idea;
- real app is raised to 20 % so the product is visibly real (and so the AI part never stands in for the product);
- motion stays the backbone because it is free, exact and on-brand.
